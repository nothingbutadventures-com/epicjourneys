How it works

You own the sales. We handle the operations.

Group travel pays well, but hotel contracts, coaches, visa and permit paperwork and midnight emergencies are exhausting. As an EpicJourneys partner you skip all of that.

You do

  • Be the trusted travel desk in your town
  • Book families, seniors and women’s groups onto our tours
  • Host groups in your lounge before they board
  • Keep your commission, locked the moment a booking is made

We do

  • Coaches, flights, hotels and meals
  • Guides, tour captains and on-trip support
  • Visas, permits and every itinerary detail
  • Full operational responsibility, 24×7
Partner toolkit

Everything you need to sell from day one

No travel industry experience needed. Your desk gets a complete set of tools:

Partner booking portal

Check live seat availability, keep travellers’ passport and ID details in one place, request new departure dates for your town, and print official tickets on the spot.

Travel CRM

Every walk-in and phone enquiry in one list, with automatic follow-up reminders so no lead goes cold.

Local marketing kit

Ready-made WhatsApp broadcast banners, social posts and print templates, already carrying your office address and phone number.

Priority partner desk

A dedicated line to our central booking team for route changes, custom itineraries and flight questions, answered while your customer is still at your desk.

Destination and sales training

Short courses on destinations, consultative selling and handling customers, so your existing staff become confident travel advisers.

Clear, upfront commission

Transparent commission on every seat booked through your account, with no cap on how much you can earn.

Who can apply

Three things your office needs

Every traveller should get the same comfort and safety, so we visit and check each space. To become an authorised LocalStart Lounge, you need:

01

A fixed commercial office or storefront

An accessible ground-floor or commercial space: an existing travel, tax or insurance office, a real estate firm, or a well-kept café.

02

A customer lounge with seating

Clean, comfortable seating where your booked groups can gather for about 30 minutes before the feeder coach arrives. This becomes your town’s LocalStart Lounge.

03

Clean washrooms and main-road access

Well-kept washrooms, and a frontage where our coaches and shuttles can stop safely for quick boarding.
