        Help & support

        Questions? We've got answers.

        What is a LocalStart Lounge?

        A certified partner office or café in your own town where your group meets. You wait seated, hand over your bags, and our coordinated transport takes you to the main tour.

        Who runs the tours?

        Each trip is run by a local partner who lives and works in the destination. Our team checks every itinerary, including the hotels, before it's listed.

        How do I find tours that start near me?

        In “Popular tours near you”, choose your city or tap “Use my location”. Tours are sorted nearest first, and you can filter by destination, type of trip, length, budget or month.

        Are prices per person and all-inclusive?

        Yes. Prices are per person, in rupees, and include local transport from your lounge to the main tour. Anything not included is listed on the tour before you pay.

        Do I pay the full amount upfront?

        No. Many tours can be reserved with a small deposit, with the balance paid before departure.

        Is it comfortable for elders and women travelling alone?

        Every group travels with a coordinator, the pace is easy, and we run Women's Special departures. Tell us about any mobility needs when you book and we'll suggest a suitable tour.

        What if I need to cancel?

        Cancellation terms are shown on each tour before you pay, and in our Cancellation Policy.

        My city isn't listed. What can I do?

        Request a callback using the form below. We add new partner points in Tier-2 and Tier-3 towns based on demand.

        Still have a question? Email support@epicjourneys.in or request a callback.
